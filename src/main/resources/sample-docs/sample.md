# Norris Bakery Staff Handbook

## Opening hours
The bakery opens at 6:30 AM Monday to Saturday and closes at 3:00 PM. On Sundays it opens at 8:00 AM and closes at noon. The bakery is closed on the first Monday of every month for deep cleaning.

## The sourdough starter
Our sourdough starter is named Gerald. It was started in 2011 and is fed every day at 5:45 AM, before the shop opens. Gerald is fed with equal weights of flour and water. Overnight, the starter is kept in the blue ceramic crock on the top shelf of the walk-in fridge, and it must never be left near the ovens.

## Baking schedule
Croissants are laminated on Tuesdays and Fridays. Baguettes are baked twice daily, at 7:00 AM and at 11:30 AM. The seeded rye loaf sells out by 10:00 AM on weekdays, so the morning shift bakes an extra batch on Thursdays.

## Staff rules
Every employee must wash their hands before entering the kitchen. Aprons are provided, and the color indicates role: green for bakers, red for counter staff, and white for the head baker. Phones are not allowed in the kitchen. Staff receive one free loaf per shift and a 30% discount on all other items.

## Allergen policy
The bakery handles wheat, milk, eggs, sesame, and tree nuts. The almond croissant is the only item containing tree nuts, and it is baked on a separate tray at the far end of the kitchen. Customers who ask about allergens must be shown the allergen binder kept beside the register.

## Emergency contacts
For equipment failures, call Priya Nair, the facilities manager, at extension 214. For any injury, notify the head baker, Tomas Weber, immediately and fill out an incident form the same day.
